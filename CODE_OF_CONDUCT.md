# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unauthorized disclosure of personal information, and other intimidating behavior are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation in proportion to the severity of the conduct. Reports should be handled discreetly; do not post sensitive information in public reports.
